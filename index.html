YOU are the Insider Threat Program, and in nine days you have to run a tabletop for people who outrank you, in a room where half the seats belong to departments that do not report to you.

This tool builds that exercise. Set the dials below and it assembles a facilitator gamebook of numbered sections, a private character sheet for every seat at the table, inject handouts ready to deal out, an after-action pack, and a companion slide deck. Everything prints. Nothing phones home — the whole thing assembles in your browser.

Plate I — The candidate, 08:40 Tuesday
1 · Scenario

What happened. Four to choose from; each one branches differently.

2 · Industry

Sets the organisation, the crown jewels, the regulator, and the small details that make the room believe it.

3 · Program maturity

Be honest. It changes how the facilitator frames authority questions.

4 · Duration

Sections are filtered to fit the clock. Shorter runs drop the optional beats.

5 · Roles at the table

Tick the seats you will actually fill. Questions belonging to empty seats fold into the facilitator’s copy, so the perspective is still represented.

6 · Difficulty

First-timer rewrites the hard beats in plainer terms and prints facilitator coaching. Veteran adds the distractions.

Heads up

This console needs JavaScript to assemble the exercise — everything happens in your browser, and nothing you select is sent anywhere.

What this is. A generator for insider threat tabletop exercises, presented as a numbered gamebook because that format does something useful: it forces a room to commit to a decision before it learns what the decision cost.

Design note. The look is an original homage to the branching-narrative paperbacks of the early 1980s — the cream stock, the red banner, the numbered sections. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any publisher or series.
