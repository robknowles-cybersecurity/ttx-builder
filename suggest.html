Two ways in · Both of them are email

YOU have run the exercise — or read it, disagreed with it, and spotted the thing that would not survive contact with your own programme. Either is worth an email.

There is no form here and no account to make. Nothing on this site collects anything about you, and a suggestion box that did would be a strange exception. The two buttons below open your mail client with a subject line and a short template already filled in. Delete any part of the template that does not apply.

Suggest a scenario

The scenarios that work are the ones built around a decision with no clean answer — where every option costs something, and the room has to spend it in front of everyone. If you have one, three lines is plenty.

The template asks for: the premise · the decision that makes it hard · the roles it stresses

Email a scenario idea

Report a correction

Wrong authority for a role, a legal step in the wrong order, a regulator that does not work that way, a section that points somewhere it should not. Corrections to the tradecraft are as welcome as corrections to the typography, and rather more useful.

The template asks for: the page or section · what is wrong

Email a correction

Prefer a tracker? Technical issues — a broken build, a rendering fault, a deck that will not open — can go to the issue tracker on GitHub.

What happens next. Suggestions are read, and the good ones go into the writing queue. Not every idea becomes a scenario — a tabletop needs a decision at its centre, not just an incident — but the reasoning gets a reply either way.
